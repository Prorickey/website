SuppBuddy is a pocket lab for supplements, built in a team of six at the Cube Buildathon. Supplements don't need FDA approval before they're sold, and hidden drugs, fillers, and contamination are invisible to the eye. With SuppBuddy, you mix a scoop of powder into water, drop the vial into the tester, and tap Start on your phone. An ESP32 shines four colors of light through the sample and measures what passes through and what scatters, then the readings are compared against other people's scans of the same product and checked against FDA and NIH records to produce a plain-language verdict. I designed the CAD for the enclosure in Onshape alongside James Nguyen and manufactured the 3D-printed housing that holds the vial, light sensors, and LCD.
